HOW TO PLAY

  • 🧟 Zombies walk toward you — each has a word/phrase above them.
  • ⌨️ Type the phrase exactly and press Enter (or space for single words) to shoot.
  • 💀 If a zombie reaches you, you lose health.
  • 🔥 Kill quickly to build your COMBO MULTIPLIER for bonus score & currency.
  • 🛒 Spend currency in the SHOP between waves.
  • 👹 Every 5 waves is a BOSS WAVE. Bosses require many phrases to defeat!
  • 🏆 Survive all 30 waves to win!
